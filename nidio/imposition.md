# Exponential Tax Rate Model

To create a fairer fiscal system, we can tie the marginal tax rate to an exponential function of the multiple ($x$) of the lowest salary within the firm (where $x = 1$ is the minimum wage).

## Mathematical Formula

$$y = \frac{4}{5} \left(1 - e^{-k x}\right)$$

Where:

* **$x$**: Multiple of the firm's lowest wage ($x = 1$ for the minimum wage).
* **$k = 0.15$**: Curvature coefficient.
* **$\frac{4}{5}$ (or $80\%$)**: Maximum asymptotic tax rate.

## Key Benefits

* **Incentivizes Higher Base Wages:** Firms are encouraged to raise their lowest salaries to lower the relative tax multipliers for executives.
* **Protective Cap:** The 80% asymptote prevents full tax confiscation on high earners.
* **Natural Progressivity:** Growth is steeper initially and flattens out gradually at higher incomes.
