# 2Cheval: manufacturing strategy

> **Status:** canonical · **Owns:** production route · **Up:** [Production](README.md)

The company is laying the groundwork for cylinder slicing, then goes through sand printing before metallurgy. The goal is to validate shapes and process at controlled cost.

## From concept to production

The company is laying the groundwork for [cylinder slicing technology](polar-slicer/polar-grid.md) ([prototype](https://github.com/nidionis/polar-slicer)). This technique is expensive to implement directly in metallurgy.

Printing in sand for [binder jetting](https://jlc3dp.com/fr/blog/binder-jetting-basics) is [a fair intermediate step](binder-jetting/cost.md) towards aluminium printing.

## Our vision

1. **Scaling 3D printing** — move from small prototypes to full-scale furniture manufacturing.
2. **Metallurgical conversion** — adapt the technology to produce engine components.
3. **A manufacturing ecosystem** — combine advanced 3D printing, polar rotary systems and metallurgy.
