# Manufacturing strategy

> **Status:** canonical · **Owns:** production route · **Up:** [Production](README.md) · **Lang:** EN · [FR](../2cheval/production/strategy.md)

The company validates the first prototype through **PLA printing → sand mold → casting → lathe finishing**, then prepares cylinder slicing and sand printing before series metallurgy. The goal is to validate shapes and process at controlled cost.

## First prototype

The first engine is an **unoptimized re-try**. The prototype price is **€15,000** (expect **€30,000** if the connecting rod and support are ordered). Route: PLA print (1.5 m³) → sand mold (10 m³) → casting (~3 m³ crucible) → lathe finishing (~1.40 m). Detail: [first prototype](../engines/manson/costs/prototype.md), [costs](../business/costs.md), [recycled material](../business/recycled-material.md), [workshop equipment](../business/workshop/equipment.md).

## From concept to production

The company is laying the groundwork for [cylinder slicing technology](polar-slicer/polar-grid.md) ([prototype](https://github.com/nidionis/polar-slicer)). This technique is expensive to implement directly in metallurgy. Printing in sand for [binder jetting](https://jlc3dp.com/fr/blog/binder-jetting-basics) is [a fair intermediate step](binder-jetting/cost.md) towards aluminium printing.

## Our vision

1. **Scaling 3D printing** — from small prototypes to full-scale furniture manufacturing.
2. **Metallurgical conversion** — adapt the technology to produce engine components.
3. **A manufacturing ecosystem** — combine 3D printing, polar rotary systems and metallurgy.
