# Production and 3D printing

> **Status:** canonical hub · **Owns:** manufacturing route · **Up:** [2Cheval](../README.md) · **Lang:** EN · [FR](../2cheval/production/README.md)

The route is: **validate the first prototype** through **PLA printing → sand mold → casting → lathe finishing**, then move to the **polar slicer**, **binder jetting** and series metallurgy.

- [Manufacturing strategy](strategy.md) — from the first prototype to furniture scale and engine parts.
- [First prototype](../engines/manson/costs/prototype.md) — unoptimized re-try, **€15,000–30,000** assurance.
- [Lathe model](../engines/manson/costs/lathe-machining.md) — series assumptions and margin.
- [Binder jetting](binder-jetting/README.md) — sand-printing route and BOM.
- [Polar slicer](polar-slicer/README.md) — cylindrical slicing prototype.
- [Failing scenario: lathe low volume](../archive/scenarios/lathe-low-volume.md) — few items per machine is the worst deal.

The XXL binder-jetting BOM is **€46,000–78,000**; the ~€100,000 figure includes R&D and integration. The commercial roll-up is in [business costs](../business/costs.md).
