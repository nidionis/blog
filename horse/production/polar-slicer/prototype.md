# Polar slicer — prototype status

> **Status:** prototype · **Owns:** slicer status and limitations · **Up:** [Polar slicer hub](README.md) · **Lang:** EN · [FR](../../2cheval/production/polar-slicer/prototype.md)

Homemade G-code generator, optimised for cylinders. It can fail on complex shapes; use Cura in that case.

Repository: [nidionis/polar-slicer](https://github.com/nidionis/polar-slicer)

- Homemade G-code generator.
- Optimised for cylindrical parts.
- Complex shapes may fail; Cura is the fallback.

The geometric model is in [polar grid](polar-grid.md); the central collision limit is derived in [angular limit](angular-limit.md).
