## Our Vision

Our mission is threefold:

1. **Scaling 3D Printing**: We are transforming the scale of 3D printing,
moving from small prototypes to **full-scale furniture manufacturing**,
demonstrating the versatility and industrial potential of our approach.

3. **Metallurgical Conversion**: Our ultimate goal is to convert this technology for **metallurgical applications**.
   This evolution will enable us to produce engine components with unprecedented adaptability,
   aligning with the highest standards of the future engine market.

## The Future of Engine Manufacturing

At 2cheval, we believe that the convergence of advanced 3D printing, polar rotary systems,  
and metallurgy represents the next frontier in engine production.  
By bridging these disciplines, we are creating a manufacturing ecosystem that is agile,  
scalable, and ready to meet the demanding requirements of tomorrow's automotive industries.
