# Straw injection

> **Status:** exploratory · **Owns:** injection concept · **Up:** [Rocket stove](README.md)

Target: homogeneous straw injection into the rocket stove. The original note uses "vapour-horse" (`vh`) as a communication unit, not a physical flow.

> 16 – 72 vh  
> using an homogeneous injection  
> of 2 / 0.05 = 40 Vaporhorse an hour  
> is a desired to get the efficiency of 2 horses  
> as marketing purpose

The actual fuel flow for a given power is in [fuel consumption](../../engines/manson/fuel.md); the fuel properties are in [straw](straw.md).

Background on viscosity units: [millipascal-second conversion](https://www.tableau-de-conversion.fr/blog/millipascal-seconde-utilisation/).
