# Rocket stove

> **Status:** canonical hub · **Owns:** heat source · **Up:** [2Cheval](../../README.md)

The rocket stove burns straw and supplies hot gas to the [Manson engine](../../engines/manson/README.md). It is a **heat source**, not the engine itself.

- [Straw properties](straw.md) — LHV, loose and densified density.
- [Injection](injection.md) — how straw is fed into the stove.
- [Combustion](combustion.md) — heat release and the thermal power required by the engine.

At the baseline 5 % efficiency, a 2 ch engine needs about **29.4 kW thermal**. The matching straw flow is in [fuel consumption](../../engines/manson/fuel.md).
