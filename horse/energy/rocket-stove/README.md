# Rocket stove

> **Status:** canonical hub · **Owns:** heat source · **Up:** [Energy](../README.md) · **Lang:** EN · [FR](../../2cheval/energy/rocket-stove/README.md)

The rocket stove burns straw and supplies hot gas to the [Manson engine](../../engines/manson/README.md). It is a **heat source**, not the engine itself. The engine is external-combustion: the working air never mixes with the fuel, which is why many fuels are acceptable.

- [Straw properties](straw.md) — LHV, loose and densified density.
- [Biomass calorific capacity](biomass-calorific.md) — LHV/HHV table and the 12 MJ/kg baseline.
- [Injection](injection.md) — how straw is fed into the stove.
- [Combustion](combustion.md) — heat release and the thermal power required by the engine.

At the 5 % baseline, a 2 ch engine needs about **29.4 kW thermal**. The matching straw flow is in [fuel](../../engines/manson/fuel.md). The feed limit is **30 kg in 20 min**, which caps mechanical power at **15 kW ≈ 20.4 ch at 5 %**; at 15 % the same feed allows 45 kW. See [fuel](../../engines/manson/fuel.md) and [efficiency](../../levers/efficiency.md).
