# Nuclear heat variant

> **Status:** exploratory / speculative · **Owns:** nuclear heat-source variant · **Up:** [Energy](../README.md) · **Lang:** EN · [FR](../../2cheval/energy/nuclear/README.md)

A nuclear source could replace straw as the heat input to the [M Reloaded](../../engines/manson/README.md). The studied range is **1–12 hp**, with **4 hp** as the reference point and **1 hp** as the simplest shielded package. This is not a safety case.

Any real design needs a radiation-protection specialist and a licensed safety analysis.

## Canonical points

- **Power scale** — 4 ch ≈ 3 kW mechanical. At 5 % that is ~59 kW thermal; at 15 % it is ~20 kW. See [power scale](power-scale.md).
- **Shielding** — **30 cm of lead** is the working figure for gamma. The older “20 cm” claim in the source note is superseded.
- **Neutrons** — lead alone is not sufficient for fast neutrons; a hydrogenous material is needed. This is the limiting factor for a fission source.
- **Geometry** — the Manson piston/heat exchanger must fit the [sizing](../../engines/manson/sizing.md) envelope; the nuclear source does not change the engine sizing.
- **Decay** — the source loses power over time; it is a heat source, not an explosive.
