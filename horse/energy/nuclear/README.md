# Nuclear heat variant

> **Status:** exploratory / speculative · **Owns:** nuclear heat-source variant · **Up:** [2Cheval](../../README.md)

A nuclear source could replace straw as the heat input to the [M Reloaded](../../engines/manson/README.md). The attractive scale is small: roughly **1–12 hp**, not a power plant. This is not a safety case.

Any real design needs a radiation-protection specialist and a licensed safety analysis.

## Canonical points

- **Power scale:** 4 ch ≈ 3 kW mechanical. At the 5 % baseline that is ~59 kW thermal; at 15 % optimised it is ~20 kW. See [power scale](power-scale.md).
- **Shielding:** 30 cm of lead is the working figure for gamma shielding. The older "20 cm" claim in the source note is superseded.
- **Neutrons:** lead alone is not sufficient for fast neutrons; a hydrogenous material is needed. This is the limiting factor for a fission source.
- **Geometry:** the Manson piston/heat exchanger must fit the [sizing](../../engines/manson/sizing.md) envelope; the nuclear source does not change the engine sizing.
