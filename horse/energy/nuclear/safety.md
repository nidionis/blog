# Nuclear safety distances / Distances de sécurité

> **Status:** preliminary summary · **Owns:** safety summary · **Up:** [Nuclear variant](README.md)

**FR** — La note source analyse un galet de **200 g** de matière radioactive vieille de deux semaines, non blindé, à 500–600 °C. Le tableau ci-dessous est un résumé, pas une procédure de sécurité.  
**EN** — The source note analyses a **200 g** highly radioactive pellet, two weeks old, unshielded, at 500–600 °C. The table below is a summary, not a safety procedure.

| Distance | Thermal risk / Risque thermique | Radiological risk / Risque radiologique | Verdict / Verdict |
|---:|---|---|---|
| Contact (0 cm) | Instant carbonisation | Thousands of Sv/h | A fraction of a second can be lethal |
| 5 cm | Intense radiant heat | Tens to hundreds of Sv/h | A few seconds can be lethal |
| 2 m | Not perceptible | A few Sv/h | Minutes can accumulate a lethal dose |
| 10 m | Not perceptible | mSv/h to tens of mSv/h | Immediate death unlikely; long-term risk remains |

A few tens of centimetres of lead can isolate the gamma component; neutron shielding is a separate problem. See the [raw safety note](notes/danger.md) and the [shielding discussion](notes/radio-isotope.md).  
Quelques dizaines de centimètres de plomb peuvent isoler la composante gamma ; le blindage neutronique est un problème séparé.
