> **Status:** source note (EN) · **Not normative** · **Canonical summary:** [nuclear variant](../README.md)

If the Manson engine piston is made of lead and has a minimum thickness of 30 cm at all points,
Wich is the case from a [medium](../../../engines/manson/sizing.md) size (~1m3)
A nuclear volume can be adapted in accordance with current regulations.  
The [horsepower](../power-scale.md) range is manageable, certainly up to around 12 horsepower,
Radioactivity should be checked
