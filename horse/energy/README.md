# Energy

> **Status:** canonical hub · **Owns:** heat sources · **Up:** [2Cheval](../README.md) · **Lang:** EN · [FR](../2cheval/energy/README.md)

This layer answers one question: where does the heat come from? The reference source is straw burned in a rocket stove. Solar and nuclear are exploratory variants documented separately.

- [Rocket stove](rocket-stove/README.md) — straw, injection, combustion.
- [Biomass calorific capacity](rocket-stove/biomass-calorific.md) — LHV/HHV table and the 12 MJ/kg baseline.
- [Solar heat](solar/README.md) — collector area, concentration and the day/night problem.
- [Nuclear variant](nuclear/README.md) — speculative heat source, not a safety case.

The heat source supplies thermal power; the engine converts it. At the 5 % baseline, a **2 ch** engine needs about **29.4 kW thermal**. The 30 kg / 20 min feed limit caps the engine at **15 kW ≈ 20.4 ch at 5 %**; at 15 % the same feed allows 45 kW. See [fuel](../engines/manson/fuel.md) and [efficiency](../levers/efficiency.md).
