# Business

> **Status:** canonical hub · **Owns:** commercial model and customer promise · **Up:** [2Cheval](../README.md)

The 2Cheval sells a hackable heat engine that runs on straw. This folder holds the commercial layer, not the engineering numbers.

- [Business model](business-model.md) — value sharing and group decisions.
- [Customers](customers.md) — who the engine is for and one consistent use case.
- [Costs](costs.md) — roll-up of production, tooling and sale prices.
- [Recycled material](recycled-material.md) — raw-material cost for a massive motor.
- [Contact](contact.md).
