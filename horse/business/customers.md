# Customer use case

> **Status:** canonical · **Owns:** customer use case · **Up:** [Business](README.md) · **Lang:** EN · [FR](../2cheval/business/customers.md)

Reference customer case: about **1.5 kg of straw for 10 minutes at 2 ch**, with 5–15 % overall efficiency.

- **Input** — about **1.5 kg of [straw](../energy/rocket-stove/straw.md)** for a 10 min run at 2 ch (≈ 8.8 kg/h at 12 MJ/kg, see [fuel](../engines/manson/fuel.md)).
- **Efficiency** — **5–15 %** customer range; 20 % is a laboratory ceiling, not a promise. See [efficiency](../levers/efficiency.md).
- **Output** — 2 ch ≈ 1.47 kW shaft, plus roughly 29 kW of heat at the 5 % baseline. Dimensions in [sizing](../engines/manson/sizing.md).
- **Comparison** — one strong horse, or a small motorcycle. Not a car.
- **Replacement** — an air-water heat pump, 15–30 kW (€15,000–35,000, heat only) or a compact tractor, 20–40 hp (€25,000–60,000, mechanical work). See [what the engine replaces](costs.md#4-what-the-engine-replaces).
- **Resilience** — the same engine can serve a heat load, a shaft load or both; see [resilience](resilience.md).
