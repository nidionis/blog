# Expecting Sustainable Scale of Energy Usage

> **Status:** canonical · **Owns:** customer use case · **Up:** [Business](README.md)

Reference customer case: about 1.2 kg of [straw](../energy/rocket-stove/straw.md) for 10 min at 2 ch, with 5–15 % efficiency.

- **Input**: about **1.2 kg of [straw](../energy/rocket-stove/straw.md)** for a 10 min run at **2 ch** (≈ 7.1 kg/h, see [fuel consumption](../engines/manson/fuel.md)).
- **Energy [yield](../engines/manson/thermodynamics.md) range**: 5 – 15 %.
- **Output**: 2 ch ≈ 1.5 kW; dimensions in [sizing](../engines/manson/sizing.md).
- **Comparison**: the power of your grand parents' car.
