## Reading

- **BOM vs all-in:** component BOM excludes R&D, software, integration, facilities and commissioning unless stated.
- **DIY vs purchased:** DIY can reduce cash cost but adds engineering time, iteration risk and commissioning effort; purchased equipment can reduce schedule risk but increases capex.
- **Series lathe:** treated as separate series capex amortized over volume, not as entry workshop equipment for a first prototype.
