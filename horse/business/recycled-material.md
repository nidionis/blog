# Recycled material cost for a massive motor

> **Status:** canonical · **Owns:** recycled-aluminium raw-material estimate · **Up:** [Business](README.md)

Raw-material estimate for a massive motor: 400,000 cans, about €4,000, excluding machining, binder, energy and labour.

## Production parameters

- **Density requirement**: 200 000 cans per m³
- **Volume per unit**: 2 m³ per massive motor
- **Total cans per motor**: 400 000 cans

## Cost breakdown

- **Unit cost**: €0.01 per can
- **Total raw-material cost**: **€4 000** per motor

This is raw material only; machining, binder, energy and labour are excluded. The full cost picture is in [costs](costs.md).
