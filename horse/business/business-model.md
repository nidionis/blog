# Business model

> **Status:** canonical · **Owns:** value-sharing model · **Up:** [Business](README.md)

We expect **20 %** of the value you make using our work, with a half-life of 1 year. Group decisions use a strict majority / Condorcet-style winner.

Group decisions use a strict majority / winner method, like [Condorcet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Condorcet_method), as specified in [iDeal](../../iDeal.md).
