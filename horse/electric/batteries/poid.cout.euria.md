# Energy cost and weight analysis (Sodium-ion battery)

## 1. Weight-cost table for usage scenarios
*Base: 100 W continuous consumption. Density: 140 Wh/kg.*

| Scenario | Duration | Required energy (Wh) | Estimated weight (kg) | Cost (100 €/kWh) | Cost (50 €/kWh - industrial) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Short use** | 20 min | 33.3 Wh | ~0.24 kg | 3.33 € | 1.67 € |
| **Medium use** | 2 hours | 200 Wh | ~1.43 kg | 20.00 € | 10.00 € |
| **Long use** | 24 hours | 2 400 Wh (2.4 kWh) | ~17.14 kg | 240.00 € | 120.00 € |
| **Fortnightly use** | 7 days | 16 800 Wh (16.8 kWh) | ~120.00 kg | 1 680.00 € | 840.00 € |

> **Weight note:** The weight indicated concerns only the battery cells. A complete system (BMS, housing, cables) adds about 15–20 % of additional mass.

### Profitability analysis
At 100 W (i.e. about **0.136 ch**), your actual costs will be about **7.3 times lower** than the "1 horse" column above.
*   **Real cost for 100 W for 24h:** ~24.00 € (Scenario 100€/kWh) or ~12.00 € (Scenario 50€/kWh).
*   **Sodium advantage:** Unlike Lithium, sodium cost remains stable (abundant raw material), making the 50 €/kWh scenario durable over the long term, unlike lithium subject to market fluctuations.
