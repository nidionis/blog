# First prototype — assurance and route

> **Status:** canonical prototype route · **Owns:** first prototype route · **Up:** [M Reloaded](README.md) · **Lang:** EN · [FR](../../../2cheval/production/prototype.md)

The first prototype is an **unoptimized first re-try**. Its investment assurance is **€15,000–30,000**, anchored to the **2-full-cylinder material stress test**: 12,596 kg of recycled aluminium, 933,053 cans at €0.03 plus separate remelting = **€28,842**, rounded to **€30,000**. The summary table is in [business costs](../../../business/costs.md#2-first-engine-prototype).

## 1. Manufacturing route

1. **PLA printing** — pattern for a 1.5 m³ envelope; lost or reusable depending on mold strategy.
2. **Sand mold** — fine sand mold (order of 10 m³) around the pattern.
3. **Casting** — molten aluminium in a ~3 m³ crucible (contract foundry or solar furnace, if available). The 66 % fill keeps remelting below the 2-full-cylinder maximum.
4. **Lathe finishing** — 2 m lathe for finishing, ~1.40 m diameter parts as needed.
5. **Assembly** — piston, connecting rod (3 high-end bearings), cylinder, fittings.
6. **Engineering and iteration** — geometry validation, fit, sealing and combustion integration.

The lathe is for **finishing and validation**, not for amortizing a whole machine on one engine. Using a production lathe for a few prototype units is a [failing scenario](../../../archive/scenarios/lathe-low-volume.md).

## 2. What the assurance covers

- Material: recycled aluminium from cans, at €0.03/can plus remelting.
- Process: sand mold, casting, finishing.
- Iteration risk: the first geometry will not be optimal; the range includes one re-try.
- It is an **assurance of investment**, not a committed subcontract quote.

## 3. What it replaces

At **€19,999**, the series product sits in the heat-pump price range (€15,000–35,000, heat only) and below a compact tractor (€25,000–60,000), while delivering ~1.5 kW shaft + ~29 kW heat at 5 %. The 5 % efficiency is **not** a heat-pump COP; the comparison is price positioning, not thermodynamic equivalence. See [business costs](../../../business/costs.md#4-what-the-engine-replaces) and [resilience](../../../business/resilience.md).
