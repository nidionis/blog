# First prototype — price and route

> **Status:** canonical prototype route · **Owns:** first prototype price and route · **Up:** [Manson costs](README.md) · **Lang:** EN · [FR](../../../2cheval/production/prototype.md)

The first prototype is an **unoptimized first re-try**. The exposed prototype price is **€15,000**; **expect €30,000 if the connecting rod and support are ordered**. Material and iteration remain major drivers (see [recycled material](../../../business/recycled-material.md) and [can cost scenarios](../../../business/recycled-material.md#4-can-cost-scenarios)).

## 1. Manufacturing route

1. **PLA printing** — pattern for a 1.5 m³ envelope; lost or reusable depending on mold strategy.
2. **Sand mold** — fine sand mold (order of 10 m³) around the pattern.
3. **Casting** — molten aluminium in a ~3 m³ crucible (contract foundry or solar furnace, if available). Fill and can assumptions are env-configured.
4. **Lathe finishing** — 2 m lathe for finishing, ~1.40 m diameter parts as needed.
5. **Assembly** — piston, connecting rod, support/bearings, cylinder, fittings.
6. **Engineering and iteration** — geometry validation, fit, sealing and combustion integration.

The lathe is for **finishing and validation**, not for amortizing a whole machine on one engine. Using a production lathe for a few prototype units is a [failing scenario](../../../archive/scenarios/lathe-low-volume.md).

## 2. What the price covers

- **€15,000** prototype base (process, material, finishing, iteration).
- **€30,000** provision when the **connecting rod and support** are ordered/supplied as custom parts.
- Prototype gearing, if required, is a separate item: see [costs](../../../business/costs.md) and [workshop equipment](../../../business/workshop/equipment.md).

## 3. Positioning

At **€19,999**, the series product sits in the heat-pump price range (€15,000–35,000, heat only) and below a compact tractor (€25,000–60,000), while delivering ~1.5 kW shaft + ~29 kW heat at 5 %. The 5 % efficiency is **not** a heat-pump COP; the comparison is price positioning, not thermodynamic equivalence. See [costs](../../../business/costs.md#4-what-the-engine-replaces) and [resilience](../../../business/resilience.md).
