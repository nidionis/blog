# Manson engine

> **Status:** canonical hub · **Owns:** engine choice · **Up:** [2Cheval](../../README.md)

The [Manson engine](https://moteurstirling.com/mace/manson/principes.php) is chosen for its simple construction and robustness. Open cycle, atmospheric pressure.

## How it works

The [efficiency](thermodynamics.md) of the engine is driven by the temperature difference between the hot source and ambient. Unlike other cycles, the [combustion volume](../../energy/rocket-stove/combustion.md) affects available [power](fuel.md) but not directly the thermodynamic efficiency.

Expected efficiency: 15 % with some [optimisation](optimisation.md), 5 % in less good conditions.
