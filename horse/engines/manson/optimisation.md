# Manson optimisation

> **Status:** exploratory · **Owns:** port-timing and porosity ideas · **Up:** [M Reloaded](README.md) · **Lang:** EN · [FR](../../2cheval/engines/manson/optimisation.md)

Ideas for port timing and displacer porosity. These are design hypotheses, not measured results. The [efficiency lever](../../levers/efficiency.md) explains what a gain is worth.

## Optimisation principle

- Compare ports at different diameter ratios (1/5 to 1/9), with a cylinder wider than tall.
- Design displacer porosity so the working air can move through the displacer without a large pressure drop.

## Hot-air exhaust port

- Mechanical control tied to the connecting-rod position during cooling.
- Open early enough to avoid pumping losses, late enough to keep the expansion.

## Cold-air intake port

- Maximum area.
- Fast, short opening.
- Solution: a notch in the piston at its lowest entry point.

## What to measure

Filling, pressure drop, indicated work and noise. A port that looks good on a drawing can lose more than it gains if it does not fill the chamber. See [frequency](../../levers/frequency.md) for the speed trade-off.
