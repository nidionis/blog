# Manson optimisation

> **Status:** exploratory · **Owns:** port-timing and porosity ideas · **Up:** [M Reloaded](README.md)

Ideas for port timing and displacer porosity. These are design hypotheses, not measured results.

## Optimisation principle

Model comparison:

- ports at different diameter ratios (1/5 to 1/9), with a cylinder wider than tall;
- displacer porosity design.

## Hot-air exhaust port

- a mechanical control tied to the connecting-rod position during cooling.

## Cold-air intake port

- maximum area;
- fast, short opening;
- solution: a notch in the piston at its lowest entry point.
