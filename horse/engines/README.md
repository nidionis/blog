# Engines

> **Status:** canonical hub · **Owns:** heat-to-motion conversion · **Up:** [2Cheval](../README.md)

This layer turns heat into motion. The **M Reloaded** is the chosen engine: open cycle, atmospheric pressure, simple construction. The **Stirling Beta** is a studied alternative: closed cycle, pressurised gas.

- [M Reloaded](manson/README.md) — chosen engine, sizing, fuel, thermodynamics, optimisation, costs, CAD.
- [Stirling Beta](stirling/README.md) — studied alternative, not chosen.

Do not mix the assumptions of the two engines.
