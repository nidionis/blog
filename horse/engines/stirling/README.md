# Stirling Beta — studied alternative

> **Status:** exploratory hub · **Owns:** alternative engine study · **Up:** [2Cheval](../../README.md)

The Stirling Beta is a **closed-cycle** alternative, studied with nitrogen and a linear alternator. It is **not** the chosen engine: the [M Reloaded](../manson/README.md) is simpler to build and uses an open cycle.

Do not mix these assumptions with the Manson numbers.

- [Beta sizing](beta-sizing.md) — volume, frequency, pressure and ΔT study.
- [Linear alternator](linear-alternator.md) — magnet/coil ranges and control.
- [Raw notes](notes/) — source derivations and links.

## Why keep it

The Stirling study is useful as a comparison: closed cycle, pressurised gas, higher complexity, possible linear generator. It documents what was considered and why the Manson was preferred.
